From observation to belonging.

A collaborative process, at once rigorous and intuitive. Each stage continues the conversation from day one.

Step 1

Listen

Understanding the site, the people and how the place should be lived in.

Step 2

Interpret

Testing ideas through diagrams, proportion and early spatial studies.

Step 3

Shape

Developing architecture, interiors and materials together, so the experience stays coherent.

Step 4

Refine

Resolving the essential details and coordinating the work that brings them to life.

Step 5

Inhabit

Handover is the beginning. We return to see how people make the place their own.
